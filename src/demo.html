FROM REFERENCE TO PERFORMANCE

The character behind the controls.

Shape & movement

The supplied photo guides the dome, broad collar, dark shell, rounded leg sections and heavy feet. The short alternating gait is an animation interpretation of a performer inside a rigid costume; the timing is tunable rather than a measured film reconstruction.

Voice & light

A short original Forbidden Planet recording is used, with captions. No synthetic voice. A short synthesized mechanical register cue precedes the unaltered original recording; this cue is an approximation, not film audio. The blue tubes light only during speech and follow its audio level when available. Licensed replica documentation supports the speech lights and independently moving head mechanisms.

The vehicle

The vehicle uses the silver body, two windshields and opening front gate in your reference. Robby enters through the front and stays standing while the gate closes around him. He drives in circles across the open test ground; choose Exit & walk to stop, open the front and let him step out upright. The boarding, circular route and exit choreography are animation interpretations.
